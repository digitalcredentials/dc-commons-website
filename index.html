DCCDigital Credentials Commons

Help build the commons for trusted credentials.

Founded in 2018 at MIT Open Learning by leading universities across North America, Europe, and Asia, Digital Credentials Consortium (DCC) built a trusted, shared infrastructure for issuing, storing, displaying, and verifying digital credentials for education and workforce. DCC now has a new name and a new home: the Digital Credentials Commons at Strada Education Foundation, built to power learner mobility between education and the workforce.

At the center of this work is a simple goal: empowering learners to have real access to and control over their credential data, so they can store and share their achievements wherever opportunity takes them.

As a Commons, we're connecting institutions, organizations, platforms, and companies across the digital credentialing ecosystem. Together, we will scale our services and continue to advance the understanding and use of privacy-enhanced, verifiable, data-rich digital credentials through open source technology development and leadership, research, and advocacy.

Educators, technologists, researchers, policymakers, and lifelong learners, help us shape what comes next. All are welcome.
